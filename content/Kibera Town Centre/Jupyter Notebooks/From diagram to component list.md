The first source provides a diagram of a wastewater treatment plant. Here is a description of the plant based on the diagram:

●

Wastewater enters the plant from various sources, including toilets, showers, and drains.1

●

The wastewater first flows into a Sewer Manhole.1

●

From the Sewer Manhole, the wastewater is pumped into an Anaerobic Septic Tank.1

●

After the Septic Tank, the wastewater flows to a Rubber Lined Holding Tank.1

●

The next step is a Four Transfer Pump, which moves the wastewater to a Slow Sand Filter.1

●

After the Slow Sand Filters, the wastewater is held in the Recycled Water Tank.1

●

From there, the water can either be pumped to a P-RW2 pump or sent through a "Bypass to Mezzanine Tanks."1

●

The P-RW2 pump sends the water to a Thickening Filter, while the bypass sends it to Mezzanine Tanks.1

●

The Thickening Filter is followed by two Up-Flow Filters.1

●

After the Up-Flow Filters, the water flows to the P-Eg1/2 pump.1

●

Finally, the water is pumped to a Roughing Filter, then to a P-URF1/2 pump, and finally to a UV Filter.1

The diagram also shows several sensors and monitoring points throughout the system, including:

●

Level sensors in the various tanks and filters.1

●

Current sensors on the pumps.1

●

A turbidity sensor in the Recycled Water Tank.1

The diagram also notes that manual tests are conducted for bacteria, chlorine, pH, and fluoride.1
